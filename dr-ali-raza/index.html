About Me

The Story Behind a Mentor Dedicated to Excellence in Medicine

Dr. Muhammad Ali Raza is a board‑certified physician in Pulmonary Medicine, Critical Care Medicine, and Sleep Medicine, with decades of global clinical and leadership experience. He currently serves patients across the North Dallas area, delivering high‑acuity pulmonary and critical care services with a patient‑centered, evidence‑based approach.

Dr. Raza has held senior leadership roles at world‑class institutions including the Cleveland Clinic (USA), Mafraq Hospital (Abu Dhabi), and the University of South Carolina, where he established the institution’s first Pulmonary Hypertension Program. His clinical interests include pulmonary hypertension, asthma management, and advanced non‑invasive ventilation strategies for sleep apnea, particularly in patients with heart failure.
