What You’ll Gain

Dreaming of becoming a Physician Assistant? We’re here to help you turn that dream into a clear, achievable path. Our Pre-PA Mentorship program is designed to guide you every step of the way from understanding what it takes to be a PA, to building a standout application that gets noticed.

Our mentors have been where you are and they’ll help you navigate the process with clarity, confidence, and strategy.

Application & Career Planning

Get expert guidance on school selection, CASPA prep, and building a standout application that reflects your strengths.

Experience & Skill Building

Learn how to gain meaningful patient care hours, secure strong letters of recommendation, and prepare for interviews with confidence.

Personalized Mentorship

Receive one-on-one support from experienced PAs who help you navigate every step from planning to acceptance.

Our Practicing PA Mentorship Services

Career Consultation

Work with experienced mentors to develop a personalized roadmap for long-term career growth. Receive guidance on specialty development, skill acquisition, and strategic milestones for sustained success.

Business and leadership guidance

Receive mentorship on leadership skills, entrepreneurial ventures, and potential clinic ownership. Learn strategies for managing teams, finances, and operations to expand your professional impact.

Research guidance

We assist in identifying research projects, contributing effectively, and navigating publication opportunities. This strengthens your academic profile, opens doors for collaboration, and enhances career credibility.

Specialty insider support from PAs

Get mentorship on transitioning into your specialty, including practical tips for new grad success, patient management strategies, and workplace navigation, ensuring a smooth and confident start.

Contract Negotiations

We provide detailed contract reviews and teach negotiation strategies through individual guidance and group workshops. This empowers you to advocate for fair compensation, benefits, and working conditions.

Resume building

Receive personalized guidance to create resumes and cover letters that stand out. We help highlight your skills, experiences, and accomplishments, ensuring your application materials are professional and compelling.

Specialty-based training

Participate in in-depth, structured training modules for your chosen specialty. These sessions enhance practical skills, clinical decision-making, and confidence, giving you an edge in patient care and career advancement.

Shadowing Opportunities

We provide specialty-focused shadowing opportunities paired with mentorship from experienced PAs. This helps you gain insider knowledge, refine your clinical skills, and build meaningful professional connections.

Specialty Selection

Through one-on-one mentorship, we evaluate your strengths, goals, and interests to help you select or transition to the specialty that fits you best. This ensures your career trajectory aligns with long-term growth and satisfaction.
