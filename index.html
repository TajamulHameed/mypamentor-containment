Common Questions

Most Popular Questions

Got questions? You’re not alone, here’s what most future PAs ask us first.

What is My PA Mentor?

My PA Mentor is a mentorship platform that supports Pre-PA students, PA students, and practicing PAs at every stage of their career. From getting into PA school to excelling in clinical rotations to advancing into leadership or new specialties, we provide structured mentorship, personalized coaching, and real-world opportunities to help you thrive.

How does My PA Mentor work?

Simply select your journey,Pre-PA, PA Student, or Practicing PA then choose the services that match your goals. Once you sign up, you’ll receive a confirmation email, and a qualified mentor will reach out within 2–3 business days to begin your mentorship.

Who are your mentors?

Our mentors are certified PA's in various specialties & PSS students helping other PA students.

How are mentors matched with mentees?

Matching is personalized. We consider your journey, goals, preferred specialties, and availability. If you have a specific interest like dermatology, emergency medicine, or surgery we’ll connect you with a mentor in that area whenever possible.

How much does mentorship cost?

Pricing depends on your stage and chosen service. Here's are te estimated cost breakdown:

Pre-PA: $85–$200 per session

PA Students: $100–$300 per service

Practicing PAs: $150–$800 depending on specialty and depth of coaching

You can view all pricing on our Services page, and bundle multiple services for discounted mentorship packages.

Is there a tuition or membership fee?

No monthly tuition or membership fees you only pay for the services you select. We believe in transparent, pay-as-you-go mentorship so you can tailor your experience to your exact needs.

How do I pay?

We accept all major debit/credit cards and PayPal. Payments are processed securely at the time of booking.
