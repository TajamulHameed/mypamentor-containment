We are a community-driven mentorship platform designed for aspiring, current, and practicing Physician Assistants. From CASPA applications and clinical rotations to career advancement and specialty transitions, we provide structured guidance, clinical exposure, and leadership training.

At My PA Mentor, you’ll find everything you need to thrive all in one place.

Core Values

Accessibility

Mentorship without barriers. We connect you to the right mentor at the right time whether you’re a Pre-PA, student, or practicing provider

Growth

Evolving with confidence. From CASPA prep to leadership coaching, we support continuous personal and professional growth.

Collaboration

Professional Excellence

Community

Mission & Vision

Closing the Gap in PA Mentorship

Our Mission

To empower Pre-PA students, PA students, and practicing PAs with personalized mentorship, career support, and specialty training. We help every PA not just succeed but rise above the standard and grow into a confident, exceptional leader.

Our vision

Our vision is to build the most collaborative PA community in the U.S. A place where every stage lifts the next. Pre-PAs learn from students, students learn from practicing PAs, and providers mentor new grads while advancing their own careers. What sets My PA Mentor apart?

•We’re the all-in-one mentorship hub , no more scattered resources.

•We’re the first mentorship platform that helps Certified PAs transition into new specialties our signature program and unique advantage.

• We are creating a connection between pre PAs, PA students to certified PAs. 

Together, we create a continuous cycle of growth, excellence, and leadership in the PA profession.
