Our Pre-PA Mentorship Services

Shadowing Opportunities

We connect you with practicing PAs and healthcare professionals for curated shadowing experiences. You’ll learn patient interaction techniques, observation strategies, and professional etiquette. This experience builds your confidence, strengthens your application, and gives you a realistic understanding of the PA role.

School Selection Guidance

Through one-on-one consultations, we evaluate your academic profile, career goals, and location preferences to identify the programs that best suit you. We help you create a personalized application timeline, improving your chances of acceptance and ensuring a strategic, organized approach.

Letters of Recommendation

We guide you in selecting the strongest recommenders and provide advice on highlighting your skills and experiences. Our support ensures that your letters reflect your commitment, professionalism, and suitability for PA school.

PA school Interview

Through structured mock interviews, we prepare you for both MMIs and traditional interview formats. You receive real-time feedback, strategies to manage stress, and practice articulating your strengths, helping you approach the interview confidently and professionally.

General Advising for CASPA

We offer comprehensive CASPA application support, including detailed feedback and editing for your personal statement. Our guidance ensures your essay is compelling, authentic, and tailored to reflect your experiences, motivations, and dedication to becoming a PA.
