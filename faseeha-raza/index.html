About Me

The Story Behind a Mentor Dedicated to Excellence in Medicine

I am Faseeha Raza, MMS, PA-C, a board-certified Physician Associate specializing in Medical and Cosmetic Dermatology, and the proud CEO and founder of Chic Derm & Aesthetics. I earned my Master of Medical Science in Physician Assistant Studies from Baldwin Wallace University and my Bachelor of Health Sciences from Cleveland State University.

My professional journey blends clinical medicine, aesthetics, research, leadership, and education allowing me to bring a 360° mentorship experience to Physician Assistants and students across the country.
